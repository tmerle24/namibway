A booking system your guests can trust, and your staff can actually run.

NamibWay is a Namibian travel platform, built and operated here, running in production today. We would like to build and run a booking system for your camps — starting with one property, so you can judge it against the real thing rather than against a proposal document.

01

Availability
that holds

One answer per unit and date, not several. A reservation that is only half made expires by itself and releases the bed, instead of blocking it for a guest who never came back.

02

Simple enough
to be used

A request arrives as an email with two links: confirm or decline. No login, no training, no new screen to learn. Staff who prefer a dashboard get one; staff who prefer the inbox keep working as they do.

03

We build it
and we run it

No server to buy, no licence to renew, no IT project to staff. We host it, back it up nightly, monitor it and answer the phone when something is wrong.

Let us start with one camp

One property, one season, measures agreed before we begin, and a decision at the end of it — extend, change, or walk away. We would rather be judged on a working system than on a presentation.

This does not start from zero

  • Room-level availability with holds that expire and tell the guest
  • One-click confirm and decline from a signed link in an email
  • A dashboard for staff who would rather use a screen than an inbox
  • Nightly encrypted backups to separate off-site storage
  • Automated deployments that only ship once the tests pass
  • Five languages — English, German, Dutch, French, Spanish
  • iOS and Android apps alongside the website
  • A documented public API, so other systems can read and write
  • Adapters designed for ResRequest, NightsBridge and hopeCloud

What a partnership adds to this is your inventory, your rules and your branding — not a system written from scratch while you wait.

What we propose

1

Agree the measures

Before a line is written: what would make this a success, and how it gets counted. In writing, so nobody argues about it afterwards.

2

One camp, one season

We set the property up, train whoever takes the bookings, and run it alongside what you use today. Your bookings never depend on us alone.

3

Look at the numbers

At the end of the season we sit down with what actually happened and you decide: extend it, change it, or stop.

4

Then, if it works

Rolled out property by property, under your own branding, hosted and operated by us. No big-bang cutover of everything at once.

What we would need from you

A named person who can decide. Your inventory, rates and booking rules as they really are, not as the manual describes them. And permission to talk to the people who take bookings today — they know where it breaks.

Why a supplier down the road

Same country, same time zone, on site within a day. Built for the connections your camps actually have, not for a fibre line in Europe.

Your data stays yours and can be exported in full at any time — no lock-in beyond the notice period we agree. And the money stays in Namibia.
