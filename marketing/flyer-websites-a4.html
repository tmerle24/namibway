Questions people ask

Do I need to understand computers?

No. You send us a message when something needs changing and we change it. If you can send a photograph on your phone, you can keep your website current.

I already have a domain name.

Then we move it across, or point it at the new site — whichever suits you. It stays registered in your name either way, and there is no extra charge for doing it.

Who owns it in the end?

You do — the domain, the words and the photographs. If you decide to stop, we hand all of it over rather than holding on to it. We would rather lose a customer than trap one.
