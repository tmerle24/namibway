# NamibWay — partner acquisition copy (English)

Approved copy for print and hand-out material aimed at Namibian lodges, camps, guest
farms, activity operators, guides and restaurants. Everything here is written to be
handed to a designer verbatim — see `claude-designer-prompt.md`.

Audience: the owner or manager of a small-to-mid Namibian tourism business. Often
not a "digital" person, often already annoyed by OTA commission and by channel
managers. Tone: plain, concrete, respectful, no startup jargon, no exclamation marks.
British English spelling ("travellers", "programme", "organisation").

Read `README.md` in this folder before changing anything — it lists the claims we may
and may not make.

## 2. A4 flyer — back

**Section: How it works**

1. **Claim your listing.** We have already prepared a free listing for most Namibian
   properties from public information. Claiming takes a minute and hands you the keys.
2. **Tell us how you want to be reached.** One-click email is the default and needs no
   setup. Connect ResRequest, NightsBridge or hopeCloud instead — or keep your calendar
   and rates here, and let us answer from those.
3. **Answer in one click.** Every request email carries a confirm and a decline link
   that works without logging in. Rooms we hold for you are released automatically if
   the request goes stale, so nothing sits blocked.
4. **The guest arrives prepared.** They travel with a plan — your arrival time, the
   drive that gets them to you, and what they are doing the next morning.

**Section: How this is different from a booking portal**

| | Typical booking portal | NamibWay |
|---|---|---|
| Cost to be listed | Free, but visibility is paid | Free, and free to keep |
| What the traveller gets | A list of properties to compare | One planned route, with you in it |
| Requests you receive | Every speculative search | One committed traveller at a time |
| Driving times and routing | Not their problem | Planned from real road data, so guests arrive when they say they will |
| Languages | Global | English, German, Dutch, French, Spanish — Namibia's actual source markets |
| Running the property day to day | Not what they do | A calendar, your rates, the front desk and the invoice — if you want them |
| Who answers the guest afterwards | Nobody | We do, before and during the trip |

**Section: What it costs**

Listing is free and stays free. We earn a commission only when a booking is
confirmed — deliberately set below the rates the international portals charge.
Nothing is owed on requests you decline, on requests that expire, or on guests who
book with you directly after finding you here.

## 5. Talk track for the person making the calls

Not for print — this is the spoken version, so the flyer and the phone call say the
same thing.

**Opening (20 seconds)**
"We're building NamibWay, a travel platform just for Namibia. Instead of a search
form, travellers talk to a planning assistant that builds them a whole route —
lodges, activities, restaurants, driving times — and then books it. We've already
prepared a free listing for you from public information, and I'd like to hand it over
to you so it's accurate."

**If they ask what it costs**
"Nothing to list, nothing to keep listed. We only earn something when a booking is
actually confirmed, and we've set that below what the big portals take. I'm not the
one who signs terms — I'll pass you to the person who does."

**If they say they're drowning in enquiries already**
"That's exactly the thing we built differently. A traveller can only have one live
request at a time with us, and only after they've created an account and given firm
dates. So you're not getting the same person's speculative enquiry alongside eleven
other lodges."

**If they say they have no time for another system**
"Then don't take one. The default is an email with a confirm and a decline button —
no login, no dashboard, no app. The dashboard is there if you want it."

**If they ask whether we could run their bookings**
"Yes — there is a full booking system behind the platform: an occupancy calendar,
your own rates and seasons, bookings taken at the desk, an arrivals list, and
invoices with VAT and the levy. We can set your property up on example bookings and
show you it running." Then stop. Do not quote a price for it; note the question and
pass it to Till or Adriano, exactly as with commission.

**If they ask whether guests can pay online**
The software does it and you may say so. What you may not say is that we take card
payments today — no merchant account has settled a real transaction yet. "It is
built and we are setting the merchant side up" is the honest sentence.

**If they ask how many travellers you have**
Answer honestly: we are launching and signing founding partners now. Do not invent a
number. The honest version is a better pitch at this stage than a vague one — being
early is the offer.

**If they ask about contracts, commission percentages or exclusivity**
Do not negotiate. Note the question and pass it to Till or Adriano.

## 6. Objection FAQ (back of the hand-out, or a separate A4)

**"Is this another OTA?"**
No. Portals collect properties and let travellers compare them. NamibWay plans the
trip first and books the parts of it. That is why we can afford to send you fewer,
better requests.

**"Do I have to give you my availability?"**
Only if you want to. Without a connection, we send you a request and you answer it.
With a connection to ResRequest, NightsBridge or hopeCloud, availability and
confirmation happen automatically. You can also keep the calendar with us instead —
then a room a traveller books comes off sale everywhere at once.

**"Could you run our bookings as well?"**
Yes. The same system runs the property: an occupancy calendar, your rates and
seasons, walk-in and telephone bookings, an arrivals list for the day, a folio per
stay and numbered invoices with VAT and the tourism levy shown separately. It is
quoted per property — ask and we will come back with a number.

**"What happens to a request I don't answer?"**
If we are holding a room for you, the hold expires and the guest is told. Nothing
stays blocked because someone forgot to reply.

**"Can I be removed?"**
Yes, at any time, with one email or one click. Within 24 hours, no questions.

**"Which languages do you sell in?"**
The platform runs in English, German, Dutch, French and Spanish.

**"Who owns my photos?"**
You do. Photos you upload stay yours, and you can remove them. Where we sourced a
photo ourselves, you can replace it with your own the moment you claim the listing.
